Lower Manhattan Prototype

Real building footprints and real roof heights from NYC Open Data, rendered as one merged 3D geometry. This is a test page only — it is not linked from the EcoCode site navigation, and the homepage model is untouched.

Source: NYC Open Data, Building Footprints (dataset 5zhs-2jue), Manhattan BIN 1000000–1999999, fields the_geom, bin, height_roof, ground_elevation — filtered here to Lower Manhattan (lon −74.022 to −73.985, lat 40.700 to 40.732) and projected to metres around the area centre. Heights are roof heights in feet converted to metres; where the dataset has no height, the prototype uses 4 m (counted above) rather than inventing a height. Roofs are flat and triangulated with earcut (holes/courtyards supported); there are no setbacks, facades, or textures, so this is empirical geometry, not photographic realism. Pipeline pattern follows the open-source Manhattan 3D approach (fetch → pack → merged BufferGeometry, one draw call); the dataset here was fetched fresh from NYC Open Data for this prototype, not copied from that repo.
