Company profile — EcoCode LLC

Name: EcoCode LLC  
Founders / Parent: Founded by EcoGreen Biotech LLC (CEO Charles Craun) in collaboration with NGHI.  
Website / Reference: EcoGreen Biotech (founding partner). 

Mission statement  
Design and deploy modular, scalable IoT smart systems that give municipalities, industrial operators, and waste-management providers real‑time, auditable intelligence on waste flows, emissions, pollution, and asset health—so cities and companies can reduce costs, meet regulatory targets, and improve public health.

Vision  
A world where urban services and industrial operations run with the same precision and transparency as modern software: measurable, automated, and continuously improving through connected sensors, open data, and modular software building blocks.

---

Core value proposition
- Modular IoT hardware: plug‑and‑play sensor nodes and gateways that can be mixed and matched for different use cases (waste bins, trucks, construction sites, stacks, waterways).  
- Scalable software platform: cloud-native, microservices-based platform for device management, telemetry ingestion, analytics, and APIs for third‑party integration.  
- Open-source foundations: reference implementations, device drivers, and analytics modules published in the EcoCode GitHub organization to accelerate adoption and community contributions.  
- Regulatory-grade reporting: validated data pipelines and tamper-evident logs for compliance, permitting, and public dashboards.  
- End-to-end services: pilot design, deployment, managed operations, and data-driven consulting to convert sensor data into operational savings and emissions reductions.

---

Strategic pillars

1. Product-led modularity  
   - Hardware families: EdgeSense (low-power environmental sensors), LoadTrack (fill-level and weight), FleetNode (vehicle telematics), EmisGuard (gas and particulate monitors), SiteBeacon (construction site multi-sensor arrays).  
   - Standardized connectors, power options (solar, battery, wired), and OTA firmware updates.

2. Platform & data  
   - Multi-tenant cloud with device registry, secure provisioning, stream processing, time-series DB, and ML inference.  
   - Open APIs and SDKs; data export in standard formats for GIS, ERP, and regulatory systems.  
   - Privacy-first design and role-based access controls.

3. Pilot-first go-to-market  
   - Work with municipalities, waste haulers, and industrial partners to run short pilots that demonstrate ROI (reduced route miles, lower landfill costs, emissions reductions).  
   - Offer “pilot-to-scale” packages: hardware, connectivity, analytics, and training.

4. Ecosystem & partnerships  
   - Collaborate with founding partner EcoGreen Biotech and NGHI for domain expertise, lab validation, and market introductions.   
   - Integrate with cloud providers, telecoms (NB‑IoT / LTE‑M / 5G), and local systems integrators.

Business model & revenue streams

1. Hardware sales — one-time revenue for sensor kits, gateways, and installation.  
2. SaaS subscriptions — tiered platform access (Pilot, Standard, Enterprise) with device limits, analytics, and reporting.  
3. Managed services — installation, connectivity provisioning, device lifecycle management, and on‑site calibration.  
4. Professional services — pilot design, ROI studies, regulatory validation, and custom integrations.  
5. Data monetization & insights — anonymized, aggregated datasets and benchmarking services for planners and researchers.  
6. Marketplace fees — revenue share from third‑party apps and analytics sold through EcoCode marketplace.

Pricing model highlights  
- Pilot pricing: low upfront hardware cost + short-term SaaS to lower adoption friction.  
- Scale pricing: per-device SaaS + per‑GB telemetry for high-frequency sensors; enterprise contracts for compliance modules.  
- Managed ops: monthly per-site fee for full operations.

Technology architecture (high level)

1. Edge layer: sensor nodes with local preprocessing, secure boot, and OTA updates.  
2. Connectivity: multi-protocol support (LoRaWAN, NB‑IoT, LTE‑M, Wi‑Fi, BLE) with adaptive data plans.  
3. Gateway & fog: local gateways for aggregation, edge analytics, and temporary buffering.  
4. Cloud platform: device registry, message broker, time-series DB, analytics engine, ML models, and REST/GraphQL APIs.  
5. Applications: dashboards, mobile apps for field crews, compliance reporting, and third‑party integrations.  
6. Security & privacy: device identity, end-to-end encryption, role-based access, and data retention controls.

Pilot examples and KPIs

Waste Intelligence Pilot (municipality)
- Scope: 500 smart bins + 10 collection vehicles.  
- KPIs: route miles reduced; collection frequency optimized; landfill diversion rate; cost per ton reduced.  
- Expected outcomes: 15–30% route efficiency gains; 10–20% reduction in collection costs in first year.

Construction Site Emissions Monitoring (pilot)
- Scope: 3 active sites instrumented with PM2.5/PM10, NOx, noise, and dust sensors; mobile monitors on haul trucks.  
- KPIs: exceedance events detected; time to mitigation; contractor compliance rate; community complaints reduced.  
- Expected outcomes: real‑time alerts reduce exceedance duration by 50%; improved contractor compliance documentation.

Industrial Emissions & Leak Detection
- Scope: perimeter and stack monitoring with mobile sniffers for fugitive emissions.  
- KPIs: leak detection time; unplanned downtime avoided; regulatory incidents.  
- Expected outcomes: faster detection and remediation; lower fines and insurance exposure.
