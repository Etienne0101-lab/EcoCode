Hero Section

Transforming Urban Waste Management with Real‑Time Intelligence
The Waste Intelligence Platform is an open‑source, modular, AI‑powered system designed to modernize how cities manage waste, recycling, and organics. Built for dense urban environments like New York City, it integrates IoT sensors, mesh networking, facility‑level forecasting, and machine learning to deliver a fully autonomous waste‑intelligence ecosystem.

Whether deployed at a single building, a multi‑block cluster, or across an entire borough, the platform provides real‑time visibility, predictive analytics, and actionable insights that reduce operational costs, improve diversion rates, and support climate‑positive outcomes.

---

What the Platform Does

A Complete Digital Nervous System for Urban Waste
The Waste Intelligence Platform continuously monitors, analyzes, and optimizes waste flows from the moment material enters a bin to the moment it reaches a processing facility.

Key Capabilities
- Smart Bins with IoT Sensors  
  Measure fill‑level, mass, contamination, temperature, and gas emissions.

- Mesh Network Aggregation  
  LoRaWAN + BLE‑Mesh clusters synchronize data across buildings and blocks.

- Facility‑Level Intelligence  
  Composting, recycling, and remediation hubs receive real‑time inbound load forecasts.

- AI‑Powered Analytics  
  Predict overflow, contamination, diversion performance, and facility congestion.

- Citywide Waste Digital Twin  
  A live, geospatial model of waste movement across neighborhoods and boroughs.

- Dashboards for Agencies & Operators  
  DSNY, OTI, building managers, and sustainability teams get actionable insights.

Platform Architecture

Built for Scale, Reliability, and Modularity

The platform follows a Hub‑and‑Spoke + Mesh architecture:

1. Sensor Layer (Spokes)
Smart bins equipped with ESP32 microcontrollers and multi‑sensor arrays.

2. Mesh Network Layer (Blocks & Buildings)
LoRaWAN gateways and BLE‑Mesh clusters aggregate and enrich telemetry.

3. Hub Layer (Facilities)
Composting, recycling, and remediation facilities receive predictive load analytics.

4. Cloud Intelligence Layer
TimescaleDB, Flask APIs, and ML engines process and visualize data.

5. Dashboards & APIs
Streamlit dashboards, Grafana metrics, and REST endpoints for agencies and operators.

---

Machine Learning & AI

Turning Raw Telemetry into Operational Intelligence

The ML layer includes:

- Temporal Forecasting  
  Predict waste generation, overflow probability, and facility load.

- Spatial Analytics  
  Block → neighborhood → borough waste flow modeling.

- Anomaly Detection  
  Identify sensor failures, cluster anomalies, and unexpected waste spikes.

- Contamination Detection (CNN)  
  Multi‑modal classification using sensor metadata and optional imagery.

- Unified Inference Engine  
  Real‑time predictions integrated directly into cloud ingestion services.
